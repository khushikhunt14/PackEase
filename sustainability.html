Planet First Packaging

Our Zero-Plastic & Carbon Reduction Commitments

At PackEase, sustainability is not an afterthought—it is the foundational core of how we engineer every single box, bag, and pouch.

🌳

100% FSC-Certified Paper

All virgin and recycled fibers originate strictly from responsibly managed forests certified by the Forest Stewardship Council.

🌱

Organic Soy & Water Inks

We replace harmful petroleum inks with non-toxic soy-based and water-soluble pigment inks for rich print quality without toxic runoff.

🔄

Circular Biodegradability

Our corrugated boxes and kraft bags break down naturally in soil within 90 days without releasing microplastic residue.

⚡

Solar-Powered Production

Over 60% of electricity utilized in our primary manufacturing facilities is generated via rooftop solar panels.
